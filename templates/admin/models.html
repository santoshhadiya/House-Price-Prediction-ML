Architecture Spec

1. Numeric Transformation

Strategy: SimpleImputer(strategy='median')

15 continuous and discrete features: BHK, Bathrooms, Super_Area_sqft, Carpet_Area_sqft, Floor_No, Total_Floors, Property_Age_years, Parking, Lift, Gated_Society, Distance_to_Metro_km, Distance_to_CityCenter_km, Nearby_School_km, Nearby_Hospital_km, Crime_Rate_Index.

2. Categorical Encoding

Strategy: SimpleImputer(most_frequent) + OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown='ignore')

3 multi-class categorical features: City (5 one-hot columns), Locality_Tier (3 one-hot columns), Furnishing (3 one-hot columns). Expands to 11 encoded features.

3. Random Forest Regressor

Hyperparameters: n_estimators=100, max_depth=20, random_state=42

Trained on 26 total features (15 numeric + 11 one-hot). Predicts continuous market price in INR with high fidelity (0.9856 R² on unseen holdout test split).
