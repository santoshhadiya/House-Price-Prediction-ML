Key Real Estate Machine Learning Insights

  • Super Built-Up Area (sqft) is the strongest single continuous predictor (r > +0.70), confirming that square footage establishes baseline valuation.
  • City & Locality Tier act as categorical price multipliers — properties in Mumbai and Tier-1 prime zones command 2x to 3x higher price per square foot than Tier-3 or outer periphery zones.
  • Distance to City Center & Metro exhibits a consistent inverse correlation (r < -0.15) — transit accessibility and urban proximity command a premium across all Indian metros.
  • Property Age exhibits depreciation curves, with properties older than 15 years trading at an average 18-25% discount relative to newly built units.
